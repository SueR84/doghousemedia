## Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are read, so we can improve the site. It only runs if you press Accept on the cookie banner. If you press Reject, or do nothing, no analytics code loads and no analytics cookies are set.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets these cookies:

- `_ga`, which tells one visitor apart from another. Lasts two years.
- `_ga_E9DMXMW36W`, which keeps track of your current visit. Lasts two years.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not share analytics data with Google for advertising. Videos on this site play through Vimeo with its do-not-track setting on.

We store your cookie choice in your browser so we do not ask on every page. You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings in the footer. Rejecting after an earlier Accept turns analytics off and removes its cookies.
