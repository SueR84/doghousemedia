  <div slot="b">

    centre, so a visiting doctor could see which academy suits them. Marketing had a 2026
    campaign with a new look and needed the series to fit inside it. That meant two sets of

    for a live case with a few of the visiting professors watching from the side of the lab.

    There were five of us on the crew, with lead aprons for the lab.

<CaseStep n="03" label="The edit" title="Accurate at every stage." surface="tint">
  We worked on this with the Boston Scientific team from the first planning conversations
  through to the final cut. The subject is technical, covering electrophysiology, pulsed field
  ablation and mapping software.

  Together we went through the transcripts and gave each professor their own angle, so the
  seven interviews wouldn't become the same film seven times. One on research and how the
  technology got to where it is. One on imaging and anatomy. One on teaching doctors from a
  different background. One on safety. And so on.

  <div slot="aside">
    The edit itself was involved and went step by step, between our editor, Alan, and the
    Boston Scientific team. Each episode started as a paper edit from the transcripts, then
    moved through rough cut and fine cut to picture lock, with the Boston Scientific team
    reviewing at every stage.

    With this subject, accuracy came first. Every procedural description, anatomical term and
    on-screen reference to the mapping software had to be correct before an episode moved on
    to the next stage.
  </div>

<CaseStep n="04" label="Graphics" title="Motion graphics for the series.">
  Boston Scientific supplied the brand assets for the new campaign. We built them into a
  motion graphics package for the series: animated title cards and lower thirds, with the
  same easing, timing and placement in every film so all eight read as one series.
  Graphics appear only where they support what the speaker is saying, so the interview stays
  the focus.
