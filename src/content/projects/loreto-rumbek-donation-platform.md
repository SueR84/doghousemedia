Loreto Rumbek is a primary and secondary school in South Sudan, founded by Sr Orla Treacy in 2008. It educates girls aged 5 to 20 and now reaches the wider community through a healthcare centre built on the same site.

Susan met the school's director in 2014 and the two of them worked out a donation and marketing strategy from the beginning. We then built what it needed: a branding pack covering logo, colour palette and typography, a new website, an online donation platform inside it, an email newsletter programme, and social accounts set up and handed over.

The site is now Loreto South Sudan, covering Loreto Rumbek and Loreto Aweil. The school and the healthcare centre run largely on donations made through the website and on support from abroad, so the donation path is the part of the site that has to work.
