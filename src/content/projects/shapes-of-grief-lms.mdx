  Shapes of Grief is an award-winning grief training programme, built by grief specialist Liz
  Gleeson and delivered with over thirty contributors from the field. Its first website
  could not handle sign-ups, course progression and the general flow of the course as smoothly
  as the programme needed.

  Liz also wanted the site to look like the programme it carries, rather than a template.
  The branding came from Kay at Patterns, and we designed and built around it.

<CaseStep n="02" label="What we built" title="An LMS for enrolment, payment, progress and reporting">
  {/* Animated learner journey, 19 October 2026, at Sue's request. */}
  <div slot="aside"><LmsFlow /></div>

  We moved the whole course onto a self-hosted LMS, and moved every existing
  participant with it, so nobody had to sign up again or lose their place.

  The course runs in four levels of lectures, interviews and animations from over thirty
  contributors. Each lesson marks itself complete and the next one is always the obvious step.
  It all works on a phone, because most of the people taking it fit it around clinical work.

  We also set up advanced reporting. Liz has a bird's-eye view of the training on her site: how
  many learners and courses there are, what is being completed and how each course is
  performing across all its learners, with the option to drill into any course for the detail.
