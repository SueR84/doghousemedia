<CaseStep n="01" label="The problem" title="A serious course on a shop window." surface="secondary">
  Shapes of Grief is an award-winning grief training programme, built by grief specialist Liz
  Gleeson and delivered with over thirty contributors from the field. It was being sold and
  delivered from a website builder that was never meant to teach a course: no proper
  enrolment, no record of where anyone had got to, and certificates handled by hand.

  Liz also wanted the site to look like the programme it carries, rather than a template.
  The branding came from Kay at Patterns, and we designed and built around it.
</CaseStep>

<CaseStep n="02" label="What we built" title="Enrolment, payment and progress, in one place.">
  We moved the whole course onto a self-hosted learning platform, and moved every existing
  participant with it, so nobody had to sign up again or lose their place.

  What it now does without anyone administering it:

<CaseStep n="03" label="The learner" title="One lesson at a time, across four levels." surface="tint">
  The course runs in four levels, and a learner works through lectures, interviews and
  animations from the contributors. Each lesson marks itself complete, the next one is always
  the obvious thing to do, and the levels build towards the certificates the programme is
  recognised for.

  It has to work on a phone. Most of the people taking it are fitting it around clinical work.
