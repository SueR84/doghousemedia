<CaseStep n="01" label="The brief" title="Dynamic and Ted Baker" surface="secondary">
  Dynamic is a retail design and construction management firm that designs and builds stores
  around the world, including stores for Ted Baker. Fitting out a store involves many trades
  working to the same plans, so Dynamic wanted a series of films that shows the shop
  installers, step by step, how each part of an installation is done.
</CaseStep>

<CaseStep n="02" label="What we made" title="A technical shoot over several days">
  The shoot ran over several days at Dynamic's Retail Academy in Dublin and was highly
  technical. We followed the installation as it was built and filmed the detail of each stage,
  from free-standing fixtures and signage to transformers, wiring and the finishing works. The series runs to more films than the three
  shown here, one for each stage, with on-screen text naming every step. We planned the
  filming so an installer can see exactly what is being done and in what order.
