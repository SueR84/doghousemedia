  Coroflo is an Irish company, based at DCU's innovation hub in Glasnevin, Dublin. Its
  product, Coro, is the first device of its kind in the world: a nipple shield with sensors
  built into it. While the baby feeds, it measures milk flow, the volume of the feed and the
  temperature of the milk, and shows the figures on the mother's phone.

  Coro won a Red Dot award for innovation in 2025 and was named in the Best of CES awards
  at CES 2026.

  Most parents have never used anything like Coro. Coroflo wanted a series of films that
  showed the product being used in a real family setting. Coro sits between a newborn and
  their mother during a feed, so parents need to trust it completely before they buy. The
  films show real parents fitting it, feeding with it and reading the results on their
  phone.
</CaseStep>

<CaseStep n="02" label="What we made" title="Explainer films, made with real parents and newborns">
  We filmed in Ireland with real parents and their newborn babies. Filming a
  newborn means working around feeds, sleep and whatever the baby decides to do next, so the
  shoot went at the baby's pace.

  We also made the animation, including a separate 45-second animated explainer. The three
  films run from just over a minute to just under two, and two of them were also cut
