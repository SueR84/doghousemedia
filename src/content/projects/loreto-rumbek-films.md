Emma and Alan travelled to Rumbek in November 2021 with cameras and a drone. The school and the healthcare centre raise most of what they run on through the website and through supporters abroad, and almost none of those supporters had seen the place.

This is the first of a series of films about everyday life at the school, what donations pay for, and what the students and teachers wanted to say to the people funding it.

We built a photography library for the charity at the same time, for use on the website, in social posts and in donor material. Elaine Barker compiled and edited the photographs.

Film reaches donors in a way the photographs could not on their own. It travels further when supporters share it, and email appeals carrying a film have converted better than those without.
