  basis.point is the Irish funds industry's charity. It makes grants to charities working in
  education, with a particular focus on young people experiencing poverty, and it is supported
  by patrons, volunteers and events across the industry.

  We built basis.point's first website, and we have worked with them since 2014.

<CaseStep n="02" label="What we built" title="A website the team can run themselves" surface="tint">
  The site sets out what basis.point does and who its grants support, and gives people in the
  industry clear ways to get involved: becoming a patron, volunteering and coming to events.

  Patron and volunteer sign-up forms write directly into basis.point's CRM, so registrations do
  not have to be typed in again by hand.

  The basis.point team publish their own news, galleries and event details without needing a
  developer for routine changes. We manage the server, carry out the technical updates and
  build new layouts and pages as they are needed, and we are basis.point's main technical
  point of contact.
