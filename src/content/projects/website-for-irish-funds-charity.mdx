<CaseStep n="01" label="The problem" title="A website that no longer served the charity." surface="secondary">
  basis.point is the charity of the Irish funds industry, established after the 2007 crash to fund
  education and opportunity for people who would not otherwise have access to it. We have worked
  with them since 2014. By 2021 the website was no longer serving them. Its tone was corporate
  rather than charitable, it put the industry ahead of the people the funding supports, and it was
  slow to load. Patron and volunteer sign-up forms did not feed any system, so every registration
  had to be re-entered into the CRM by hand.

<CaseStep n="02" label="What we made" title="Sign-up forms connected directly to the CRM." surface="tint">
  We rebuilt the website around the people the charity supports, so that visitors see who the
  funding helps first. Sign-up forms now write directly into the CRM, which removes manual
  re-entry entirely. We also moved the site to better hosting and reduced page load times.

  The basis.point team now publish their own news, galleries and event details, without needing
  a developer for routine changes.

  We have maintained and supported the site for nine years, which suits a small charity without
  an in-house technical team.
